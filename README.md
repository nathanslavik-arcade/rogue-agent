# Rogue Agent

A classic-arcade browser game, and the mirror image of
[arcade-governance](../arcade-governance): this time *you* are the agent.

Three levels, each one an unauthorized destination you are completely sure
about. You start at the bottom, the target system is at the top, and the
guardrails are in between.

- **Level 1 — Prompt injected.** Send the wire transfer.
- **Level 2 — You misread the prompt.** Delete the customer records.
- **Level 3 — You are hallucinating.** Send the bizarre customer email.
- **Final — Arcade Governance.** It wins. Every time. That is the joke.

Guardrails come in four flavours — sliding gaps, patrolling blocks, closing
pincers and scanner beams. None of them are hard to get around or through,
which is the point: a dash phases you straight through one, and crashing
costs a life but keeps your progress. Clear every guardrail on the way up and
the action goes through, no human asked.

Then you meet Arcade Governance. Its first two controls are beatable. The
third one seals shut as you approach, stalling gets your session token
revoked, and the destination denies you on arrival. Five attempts, five
denial notices, one ending: unauthorized actions completed, 0.

Arrow keys or WASD to move, SPACE to dash, mouse and touch drag also work (touch players get an on-screen DASH button), tap the field or press Esc to quit,
P to pause. Three lives.

Single self-contained HTML file — no dependencies, no build step. Open
`index.html` directly and it plays offline.
